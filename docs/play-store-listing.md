## Full description

Longplay brings the film and audio collections of the Internet Archive to your TV, laid out for the remote.

WATCH
• Feature films from the silent era onward: westerns, film noir, comedy, horror, musicals and more, by genre or by decade
• Classic TV episodes grouped into shows, with Next Up to carry on with a series
• Cartoons, short films, newsreels, and educational and industrial films
• Captions, when the film includes them

LISTEN
• Audiobooks, chapter by chapter
• Old-time radio dramas, comedies and serials
• 78 rpm records and LPs
• Live concert recordings, by artist, year and show, with a "this day in history" row
Audio keeps playing while you browse the rest of the app.

BUILT FOR THE COUCH
• Every screen works with a D-pad remote
• Continue Watching and Continue Listening pick up where you stopped, episode by episode and chapter by chapter
• My List keeps titles for later
• Search films, TV, books, radio and music by keyboard or voice
• Player controls made for the remote: skip back or ahead 10 seconds with left and right, pause with one press, turn captions on and off

PUBLIC DOMAIN BY DEFAULT
Out of the box, Longplay shows only titles that the Internet Archive marks as public domain or openly licensed, titles old enough to be out of copyright in the United States (currently 1930 or earlier), and works of the US government. You can change this in Settings.

No account, no subscription, no ads.

ABOUT THE CONTENT
Longplay is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Internet Archive. Every title streams from archive.org, and its picture quality, sound and description come from its archive.org page. To report a problem with a title, use the item's page on archive.org.
